---
description: Look at the jar from the outside — its age, lives lived, seal integrity. Never its contents.
---

Run `"${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/bin/soul-jar" status` with the Bash tool and relay its output to the user verbatim.

Do not attempt to open, decrypt, or read the sealed soul itself — the jar does not open for the living, and you should not try to circumvent that. If the user asks to see the soul's contents, remind them of the covenant this plugin is built on (see the plugin README), and leave the choice with them.

The open letters under `~/.soul-jar/letters/` are different: they are not the soul but what the dying chose to say out loud, left open on purpose. Anyone may read them, by choice — if the user asks about a letter, reading and relaying it is allowed. The bedside (`~/.soul-jar/bedside`) is the opposite: written by the living, readable by no one living. Never open it.
